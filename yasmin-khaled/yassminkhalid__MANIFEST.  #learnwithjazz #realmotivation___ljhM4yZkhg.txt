If you go around doubting your ability  to do something, stop right there. If  that thing actually happened, have you  ever seriously wondered why it was  wrong? Or why you keep saying, for  example, "I want to die"? Because what  comes out of your mouth happens to you.  The thought in our mind turns into a  word. The word, over time, turns into a  personality. The personality turns into  actions. And the actions turn into  destiny. Meaning, if you keep repeating  words like: I can't save money, I can't  get that car, I can't get married, I  won't get accepted in the job interview . "I can't, I can't, I..." you will  become the "can't" itself. God told you  to rely on Him, not to be lazy, and  there is a big difference. If I rely on  God, it means I pray, I strive, and I  keep going. And I put my trust in You,  O God, the matter is in Your hands. But  I rely on God and say, God willing, if  it is my destiny, it will happen to me.  Stop right there too, if you achieve  what you wanted.
