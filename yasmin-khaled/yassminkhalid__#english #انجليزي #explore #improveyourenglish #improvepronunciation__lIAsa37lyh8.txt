Let me tell you about the treasure of a  technique I used, which made me stop  procrastinating and start improving my  English instead of just scrolling  through videos or not starting at all —or in other words, just saving them  like you guys do. And what I used is  the timer technique. I mean, I always  committed myself to a specific time so  I wouldn't slack off. For example, for  writing, I would always set 15 minutes  on my phone every day. And I would  commit to writing down everything.  Everything that was on my mind. The  same goes for listening. I would make  use of the five minutes of listening  that I did every day. And I would do  shadowing with it, which is repeating  the words and sentences after the  speaker. So that, as they say, I could  kill two birds with one stone:  listening and accent, all in five  minutes. The same applies to speaking.  At first, for example, I would set a  five-minute timer. Then I started  increasing it to 10, 20 minutes, and so  on. Seriously, it's the technique that  worked best for me. Don't just save  this. Bye.
