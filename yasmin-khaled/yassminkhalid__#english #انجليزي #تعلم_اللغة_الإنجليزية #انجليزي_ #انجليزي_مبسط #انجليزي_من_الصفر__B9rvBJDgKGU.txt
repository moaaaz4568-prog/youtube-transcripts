Why listen to podcasts instead of music , and what should you listen to? You  mostly listen to music either to escape  a feeling or to trigger one; so if  you’re upset or frustrated, 100% you’ll go listen to music. It will  trigger the feeling of being upset or  frustrated. By listening to music that  matches the state you’re in. Besides,  from personal experience, music always  leaves me a bit disconnected from  reality, and this happens mostly  because we listen to music to try to  zone out of the world, creating fake  scenarios and stories; this fuels that  sense of detachment, which makes you  less productive and wanting to stay in  that state forever. The best period of  my life was when I had quit listening  to music. I was making good use of my  commute and free time. Let me show you  how. So, what should we listen to?  First off, the Andrew Huberman podcast.  I swear to you that this podcast in  particular has everything you are  looking for. Any problem you have for  which you want to hear a discussion or  logical solutions, I swear you’ll  find it here. Second, the Impact Theory  podcast. The topics covered, the way  they speak, the discussion style, and  the English in these videos are capable  of completely transforming your English  like that.
