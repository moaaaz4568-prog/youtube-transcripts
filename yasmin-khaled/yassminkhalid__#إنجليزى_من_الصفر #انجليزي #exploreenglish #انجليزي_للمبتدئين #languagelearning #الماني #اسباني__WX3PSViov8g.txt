How can I learn any language on my own  from home? Easy. First off, you need an  application or a program to introduce  you to the language you’re starting.  So you know what you’re getting  yourself into. One of the great apps  that has all languages is Busuu. Just  five minutes a day to get a good  introduction to the language you’re  learning. It follows the principle of  having you listen a lot. And that is  exactly what we need. Duolingo also  works, but I don’t like it. Moving on , the second thing is you need to learn  the most common words in the language.  So you need to listen a lot, grab your  pen and paper, and pick out words. We  don’t need courses; it’s all online . These are things to listen to every  day for German; take a screenshot for  French, Spanish, Italian, and English.  Listened and picked out words? Then we  need to make sentences. Take the five,  six, or ten words you found and go to  the YouGlish.com website. And yes, it  has all the languages you could  possibly want to learn. You type in the  word you want, and it shows you  sentences with audio. There, it made a  sentence for you, plus the video it’s  taken from. So you’ll also learn the  pronunciation. Repeat every word and  sentence nine times so they stick in  your brain. Once you feel you can form  sentences on your own and have  understood the basics well, start  practicing speaking those basics.
