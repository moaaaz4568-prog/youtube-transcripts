This is a project I worked on a while   ago. It is a complete rebranding of a   brand already in the market. Their   field of expertise is SaaS, meaning   Software as a Service. Specifically for   the e-commerce sector. The brand has   been in the market for three years. Of   course, I’m talking about the "  Mahfool" brand. The first question now,   what does Mahfool mean? "Mahfool   Maqfool" is a Saudi idiom. It means "  don't worry about a thing." Naturally,   there had to be a reason to develop the   brand. This leads us to the second   question, which is, what exactly is the   problem? The problem is that Mahfool   has very strong competitors, namely   PayTabs and 2Checkout. These   competitors have 10 to 20 years of   experience in the market. We are   talking about a field where the game is   almost. Practically decided, but the   market has gaps and needs that have   simply been completely ignored, which   is that most brands only provide   payment gateway services. And they   ignore the Arab world entirely. Or they   don't focus on it. Their platforms have   user experiences that might. Be a bit   weak. And the general look of these   brands is outdated. Suggesting it's   from the early 2000s, for example.   Alright, let's go. Let's look at the   current market situation. As I   mentioned, weak designs and user   experience. Their communication is   always general, meaning without. Focus   on a specific category. Now, the   question is, what is the solution? The   start was clear: we needed to build a   brand strategy. Now, this "strategy" is   a bit of a big word; we just mean   answering a few questions. Five   questions specifically. They are the   Who, What, How, Where, and the big Why.   Okay, let's start with the Why, as   Simon Sinek says. And the reason is   that Mahfool believes Saudi stores can   be the best in the region. After   defining the Why, let's look at the Who  . We mean who are our target customers,   and who are our competitors. Of course,   we have already talked about the   competitors. So, who are the ideal   customers for the Mahfool brand? We   created a semi-realistic persona for   Ahmed. This is Ahmed, an employee who   wants to start his own online store and   quit his job. But Ahmed has a problem:   platforms like Salla and Zid, despite   offering excellent solutions, rely   entirely on ready-made templates and   lack design freedom. At the same time,   platforms like Shopify and WordPress   offer great design freedom, but they   don't support. Local payment or   shipping methods in the Saudi market.   So here, Ahmed will see Mahfool as the   solution to his problems. Because it   shares the same values with him. Which   are simplicity, ease of use, and giving   better options. And when Ahmed searches   for the brand's story and what Mahfool   means, he will find that someone was in   the same problem as Ahmed and was able   to solve it. From here, it is Mahfool's   role to reflect all of this in simple,   easy designs. That give a sense of   strength and modernity. And communicate   brand messages centered on simplicity.   In addition to using a confident,   direct, and friendly tone of voice. By   following this strategy, Mahfool will   reach its goal. Which is to compete   strongly in the Saudi market. Alright,   let's apply all these things to the   current logo. If we compare it with the   competitors, we'll find it weak. These   lines and its shape look like a   festival or an event or something. Okay  , let's see the logo after the   rebranding process. As is clear, the   logo became strong with bolder lines   and fewer colors. And also, the icon   now tells the Mahfool story. Instead of   just an inconsistent shape like the   previous logo. And this icon is the   result of merging the letter M from the   name. Of course, with Mahfool's focus,   which is helping Saudi store owners.   Okay, let's go back and see the new   logo directly with the competitors.   From the logo alone, it's clear that   the Mahfool brand will fill the market   gap we talked about. Which is   addressing the Arab world with strong   and simple designs. The question now is  , what will the identity look like?   Naturally, by using strong and modern   primary and secondary colors. Showing   that the brand knows its field and can   dominate it. And the focus will always   be on the brand's message. Which is   simplicity and ease. So the slogans   were like "Simple to manage, simple to   scale" and others. Additionally, I   designed icons to align with the   brand's essence. Which makes the   Mahfool brand stand out in the virtual   world and on the ground. Thank you.
