Want to know if any marketing strategy is right or
wrong? Consider the concept
of friendship. Look at what happens within friendship and see if something
similar happens in business. So, let's say you met someone
at a coffee shop every day, a new person joined your group.
If you meet this person every night at the
coffee shop wanting something from them, wanting a
favor from them—sometimes you want to sell them your watch instead of
selling it elsewhere, sometimes you want to borrow money, sometimes you want to borrow
their car, sometimes they want you to do a favor—is there
any hope of this person becoming your lifelong friend? No, they won't even like you. They'll only
come around when they need something. Now, let's compare this to a
business that does
this: someone who sits with their potential customers
at the coffee shop every night wanting
something from them. This person will be the most insignificant to
the user who experiences this. Why? Because you,
on the other hand, are the one who's self-serving.
The business that acts like a true friend, the kind where you
go and chat with your friend
not for personal gain, but for mutual benefit, so the
other party can benefit—the kind of business that does this 20, 30,
40 times, for example—has a much higher chance of becoming your lifelong friend. A
lifelong friend means you won't
think of anything that requires your
lifelong friend's expertise without first asking them. The
business that thinks in terms of
friendship is the one that knows how to build a
real customer base. For them, the brand matters, not just the
transaction. It's not just about fulfilling a need; it's about being
with
the brand, standing behind it, and wearing a badge
on your chest. That's someone connected to the brand. In contrast,
someone else will only want to take advantage of the
brand. You'll go to them like you go to Zara
when they have a sale—because they're your
opportunistic friend who always wants something from you. And therefore,
You'll only go to him if you want something from him, just as you only
come to me if you want something. I wo
n't support you, I won't stand by you, I wo
n't talk about you behind your back. It's exactly like friendship, by
comparison.
