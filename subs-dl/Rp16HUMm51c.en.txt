What are the things you need to know
when starting a business, even if you're not a
marketing specialist? You can't do marketing on
your own, and no one else will be able to
market you properly. You have to understand that you have no other
option, no other prescription, than to build
your own marketing team. Anyone
who tells you otherwise, I don't know
what they're trying to say, but you can't do it alone.
Try hiring any freelancer or any marketing company
in the world; you won't get anywhere. Nothing ever
works out, but what you need will happen. So, you do
n't need to know more than that:
your prescription is to gather the minds of people and
keep them on board. They'll work for you, showcase your brand, and
bring out the best in you.
If you really weigh this in your mind and remember it, I think it will save you from a lot of
mistakes that owners make in their
first three years, and from spending a lot of money on
pointless and unnecessary travel.
