The phrase "I want to become famous" is very strange. Why is
it so nice? What guarantees that
when you become famous, you'll be truly famous? Three-quarters of
famous people are famous for the wrong reasons, famous for
negative reasons. So, the idea that a personal brand
will make me famous just by making videos is very superficial.
Since you've entered the business world, you already have
something. A personal brand is about
building a media presence around your product or service,
broadcasting it to the world. Who are you if a phrase
like that just means "Go make some
videos and hookups and become famous"? Good for you.
