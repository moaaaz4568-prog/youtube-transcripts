After you create a solid in After   Effects and import the page, you must   enable the "Continuously Rasterize"   button so you can zoom freely without   losing any quality. Then apply the "  Tint" effect to change the color to   something that matches the mood or the   client's identity, but make sure to   maintain the reverence of the Quran.   After that, highlight the verses using   a shape layer. But for the highlight to   be precise, use the rulers. Press Ctrl   + R, drag from the top and the side,   and pinpoint exactly where you want the   shading to be. This move will make it   act like a magnet once you bring in the   shape layer to draw; your selection   will be precise, like this. Then, make   the shading color match the background   or any suitable color, and change the   blend mode to "Add." Now, we enter the   animation phase. To animate the shading  , click on the shape layer and set the   anchor point to the right. Create a   scale keyframe, move forward, unlink,   and set the starting value to zero.   Adjust the timing and the graph   according to your scene. Another thing   to do is to start the scene with the   entire page visible so the viewer knows   which Surah and page this verse is from  . Now we move on to camera movement;   select the first three layers. Enable   the 3D switch. Pre-compose them. Enable   the 3D switch again. Enable "  Continuously Rasterize." Create a new   camera and a null object. Enable 3D for   the null. Parent the camera to the null  . Start using the null to adjust the   angle and the position you want to   start from. Then begin your animation.   After that, we enter the styling phase.   Return to the pre-comp. Bring back the   solid from below so you can evaluate   the look. Apply the "Bevel Alpha"   effect to the shape layer to give it   this effect from below. Next, get a   suitable texture like paper or a grunge   texture. Apply the "Tint" effect to   block any color other than black and   white. Change the blend mode by using   Shift and the plus sign until you find   something suitable. You can also apply   the "Curves" effect to strengthen the   texture further. Use the "Motion Tile"   effect if the texture is not covering   everything. For me, this is the texture   I used. And these are the settings and   effects I applied to it. After that, go   back to the main composition. Create   two adjustment layers. Go to the first   one and select the shape tool. Choose   the circle or the ellipse. Double-click   it to mask the layer. You will be left   with the edges. So, invert the mask.   Apply Gaussian Blur to give you the   focus effect. You can add Quick   Chromatic Aberration to give you. This   effect, then increase the feather to   soften the edges until it looks right.   After that, go to the second adjustment   layer.
