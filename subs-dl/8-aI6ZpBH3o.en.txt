So what should I do if I don't have much money but need an
engineer to fix and build the brand?   It's
great to find a junior engineer, a young engineer, or even an
engineer still in university. It's perfectly normal to let them
learn from you, give them space and empowerment. This is the best way to
develop their skills, and you're the one who
ultimately benefits. So, you might find a young person, maybe in their
second year of university, who loves the subject but doesn't know how to
do it or master it. This is like a young cub.
If you can't find
ready-made players, you need to go to these cubs and nurture them.
One of them might become like
Messi, even though he's not a star yet. He has the talent, the instinct.
You'll embrace him. His resources
will be minimal, his needs will be
very few, and you'll raise him up with you. Right now, his
market value might be low,
but with every leap he makes in his skill level, you have to
increase his financial value. The market won't wait for you.
If he makes a leap and doesn't get the reward from you, he'll find it elsewhere.
