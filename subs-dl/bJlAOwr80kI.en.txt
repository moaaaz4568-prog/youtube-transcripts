learning, focus, and hard work. It requires a

whole point is that when you spend long periods of time
structured your content—not improvised content, but
structured content—when you spend

to it. You understand? It becomes
their normal function. So when your brain cells become accustomed to
structured content...  When you're working on content,

This is because your brain cells aren't consciously involved in what's happening. If they know how to do something, they'll be able to do it
improvised or in a structured way. If they don't know how to
do something, they won't be able to do it either way. So,
your ability to produce substantial
content improvised only comes when your
brain cells are thoroughly immersed in the subject matter, not in a live, but in a
recorded way. If we consider improvisation to be live, then
when you apply that immersion to structured content,
you'll find it performs well in the
live improvisational part. So, yes, anyone can create
content if they work hard, study, do
their homework, develop their skills, and experiment a lot. They can
create content that will turn out well. However, when they
want to produce that content faster, with
fewer mistakes and higher quality, they sometimes need an expert.
