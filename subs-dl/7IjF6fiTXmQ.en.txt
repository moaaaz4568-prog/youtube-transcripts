Ultimately, sales psychology and
marketing are both related,
but with different goals. In sales, I want you to
take action right now, so I need to use human
psychology in a way that
makes you take action.
Marketing, on the other hand, is about making you like me, so I need to
use human psychology in a way
that makes you like me. So,
what's the sales psychology we see today? Outbound sales with closes?
That's rotten psychology. Walking around the
mall and someone giving you that is strong sales. But
picking up the phone and saying, "
Sir, we have units in this
new project for 9 million pounds, and
there are payment plans," that's nonsense.
