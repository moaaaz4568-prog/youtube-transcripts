By the end of this video, you will have   a well-known, clear roadmap for logo   design. So, let's begin. Why do I have   steps for logo design in the first   place? Let me tell you a story from   seven years ago, when I first started   designing logos as a beginner. Whenever   I received a logo design project, I   would feel a strange sensation. A   feeling of fear. Yes, I felt afraid.   That's because I didn't know how to   start. Where would I get an idea from?   How would the logo design turn out? And   especially, I was afraid of the   client's revisions. In short, I was   lost. But over the years, and after   more than 100 different logos and   projects, I created a practical plan   with known stages. I called it the Kimo   Logo Design Process. It consists of 12   steps. 12 simple yet effective steps   for designing logos. Let's start with   the first step, which is the Brief.   It's essentially a meeting with the   client. The result will be a file   containing all the answers I need to   design any logo. Number two, signing   the contract and the first payment.   Number three, Research. In this step, I   conduct comprehensive research on the   company's details and, of course, its   competitors. Number four, Sketching and   Brainstorming. In this step, I create   what is called a mind map, a mental map   that connects everything I know about   the company. Number five, Mood Board   plus Art Direction. Simply put, it’s   a collection of images that show the   client what the final result will   approximately look like. Design: 90%of   this step happens inside Adobe   Illustrator. Number six, Presentation.   After finishing the logo design and   suggesting different colors and fonts,   I present all the outputs to the client  . Step eight, Feedback; most creatives   hate this stage. I personally used to   hate this step, but over time, I   realized it is one of the most   important steps. Step nine, Revisions,   which is applying all or some of the   feedback from the previous stage.   Number 10, Final Presentation, which is   showing the final outputs to the client   and reviewing every small detail.   Number 11, Final Payment, which is a   fairly obvious step. The last step is   preparing the source files for the logo   in all formats and variations. To make   using the logo easy across all   applications. And these are simply all   the steps I follow to design any logo.   Before you close the video, if you want   to design a logo for your company using   these steps, you can contact me through   my personal website. Thank you.
